# Knowledge Article: Service Plans, Add-ons & Upgrades

**Article ID:** KB-PLAN-003  
**Category:** Broadband & Mobile Plan Questions  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-01

---

## 1. Available Broadband Plans
- **BasicFiber 100**: 100 Mbps Download / 20 Mbps Upload ($49.99/mo)
- **UltraFiber 500**: 500 Mbps Download / 100 Mbps Upload ($69.99/mo)
- **GigabitMax**: 1000 Mbps Download / 500 Mbps Upload ($89.99/mo)

## 2. Available Mobile Plans
- **5G Flex Saver**: 10GB high-speed data + Unlimited Talk/Text ($25.00/mo)
- **5G Unlimited Plus**: Unlimited high-speed 5G data + 15GB Hotspot ($45.00/mo)

## 3. Plan Change Rules
- Plan upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing.
- Plan downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
- Early contract termination fee applies if downgraded during 12-month lock-in period.
