# Plan Upgrade

**Article ID:** PLAN-001  
**Category:** Plans  
**Keywords:** plan, upgrade, package, subscription, speed, broadband, mobile, tier  

---

## Purpose
This article provides guidelines for agents handling customer questions regarding plan upgrades, package options, bandwidth tiers, and subscription modifications.

---

## Procedure

### 1. Verification Requirements
- **Verify Current Plan**: Inspect customer record to identify current plan name and monthly fee.
- **Verify Account Status**: Ensure customer account is active and billing status is in good standing before processing upgrades.

### 2. Available Plan Catalog
- **Broadband Plans**:
  - *BasicFiber 100*: 100 Mbps ($49.99/mo)
  - *UltraFiber 500*: 500 Mbps ($69.99/mo)
  - *GigabitMax 1000*: 1000 Mbps ($89.99/mo)
- **Mobile Plans**:
  - *5G Flex Saver*: 10GB Data ($25.00/mo)
  - *5G Unlimited Plus*: Unlimited 5G Data + 15GB Hotspot ($45.00/mo)

### 3. Handling Missing Information
If customer query is vague (e.g. "I want better internet"), ask for missing details (**ASK**):
- Desired internet speed / usage type (gaming, remote work, streaming).
- Mobile hotspot or international roaming requirements.

*Strict Grounding Rule: Never invent plan speeds, prices, or custom promotional packages not listed in the official catalog.*

---

## Escalation Conditions
Escalate to specialized sales/retention team (**ESCALATE**) if:
- Customer requests custom enterprise/B2B SLA contracts.
- Customer disputes early contract termination fees during plan downgrade.
